One image summing up the world's news of Saturday 21 November 2026, made for
the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261121).

The front page leads with Russia's biggest missile and drone barrage on
Ukraine, with missiles landing in Poland, and covers four more of the day's
stories: the AI stock sell-off that pushed the S&P 500 into correction, the
COP31 climate talks deadlocked over fossil fuels, the magnitude 6.9 earthquake
in Peru's Andes, and the pancreatic cancer pill trial in which tumours became
undetectable in one in twelve patients.

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` to the output directory, byte for byte.
